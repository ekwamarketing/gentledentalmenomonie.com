Our Aesthetic Services

Facials

Refresh and renew your skin with customized facials tailored to your unique skin type and concerns. These treatments cleanse, exfoliate, and hydrate the skin, revealing a smoother, brighter complexion. Some facials also stimulate blood circulation and collagen production, resulting in long-lasting, glowing skin.

Dermabrasion

An advanced exfoliating treatment that uses a rotating device to resurface the skin for a younger, smoother appearance. This FDA-cleared treatment can help reduce the appearance of acne scars, uneven skin, age spots, and fine lines. It’s an excellent treatment for patients who want noticeable results without surgery or prolonged downtime.

Dermaplaning

A gentle exfoliation treatment that removes dead skin cells and fine facial hair to achieve a brighter complexion and a silky-soft finish. This process instantly smooths the skin and helps skincare products absorb more effectively.

Brows & Lashes

Enhance your natural features with expert brow and lash services that shape, define, and elevate your look. The goal is simple: beautiful, balanced brows and lashes that frame your face effortlessly.

Head Spa Services

Indulge in a relaxing scalp and hair treatment designed to detoxify, nourish, and rebalance the scalp. Head spa sessions include scalp analysis, deep cleansing, exfoliation, and a soothing massage to promote healthier hair and scalp. These services help address buildup, dryness, hair thinning, and stress while delivering a deeply calming experience.

Waxing Services

Achieve smooth, hair-free skin with professional waxing services for the face, legs, arms, and underarms. High-quality wax and gentle techniques help remove hair from the root, resulting in longer-lasting smoothness and slower regrowth compared to shaving. Whether it’s a full arm or leg wax, we design treatment to minimize irritation while leaving your skin soft, clean, and refreshed.
